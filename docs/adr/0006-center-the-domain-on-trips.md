# Separate Passenger identity from Trips

MDSweep will use a deep Passengers module for Provider-owned Passenger identity and a deep Trips module for the operational Trip lifecycle, rather than top-level Manifest Import, Dispatch, Driver Work, and Billing Export modules. A Passenger may exist independently of a Manifest or Trip; Trips references a Passenger and does not own Passenger identity. Manifest intake, planning, performance, review, and billing remain internal areas behind the Trips interface; this keeps broker details, Provider decisions, Driver-recorded facts, corrections, closure, and billing history local while allowing the Dispatcher and Driver experiences to remain distinct adapters. Access remains a separate module, and MTM CSV/XLSX readers and billing writers translate external files without owning Passenger or Trip state. This supersedes the feature-ownership shape retained by ADR 0005 without changing its layered projects, Wolverine, persistence, or web-client decisions.
